Case study 02 · Fall 2026

The Garden: my portfolio as a product

Designer and product owner

My role
Designer and product owner
Built with
Figma, Clip Studio Paint, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Status
Live at myeminme.github.io

I drew the art, designed the interactions and content, and directed the build. The code was written with AI-assisted coding.

The problem

A portfolio has two audiences: people who want to enjoy the art, and recruiters who have about a minute.

I wanted a site that feels like my illustrations and still gets people to the work fast.

The idea

The home page is an illustrated garden. The three signs are the menu: About Me, Portfolio, and Contact. On phones the same three options sit as buttons at the bottom of the screen.

Clicking my character zooms in and starts a visual-novel style chat. You can ask me questions, flip through my journal, or take a photo with me.

Who it’s for and what came first

  • Two users: visitors who want to explore the art, and recruiters who need to find relevant work quickly.
  • The garden, signs, and portfolio came first. The chat, journal, and photo booth were extras that make the site memorable.
  • Once I chose my target roles, the next priority was the recruiter path: the hiring switch, case studies, and these three role-specific sites.

Fixing performance

The first version lagged badly in Chrome, even the cursor. The cause was the illustration itself: large vector layers were being redrawn on every frame while leaves and flowers animated.

The fix was to split each layer into pre-rendered images plus small animated pieces, so the browser moves parts instead of redrawing the whole scene. After the rebuild the site ran smoothly.

Iterating from feedback

  • Tested in Chrome, found the lag, traced it to the cause, and shipped the fix.
  • Swapped Instagram for email and a contact form after deciding where I want to be reached.
  • Added the hiring switch and the Technica case study after deciding which roles to target.
